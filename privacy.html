Budget Bot Sheets Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Budget Bot Sheets is a private, personal tool. It accesses only the Google Sheets its owner authorizes, using the Google Sheets scope, solely to read and update the owner's own spreadsheets.

Data accessed through the app is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Nothing is stored beyond the OAuth tokens kept on the owner's own machine.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Budget Bot Sheets' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
